BOOSTER CLUB

Support from the outside.

The Y&G Booster Club is a separate parent-and-community organization that supports the club through fundraising, sponsorships, and dues. It has its own board, distinct from the student officers on the Students page.

Note: This board is separate from the student officer team. Sarah Holloway holds both the Youth & Government Club's faculty advisor role and Main Director here.
